TSUNAGU - Our Cross-Border Team

Kohomadha operates under the TSUNAGU banner: a small core in Tokyo working with a standing network of members across markets. Tsunagu is the Japanese verb "to connect" - it is what the practice is built to do.

Members and partners currently work with us from Brazil, Africa, the Middle East, and across Europe. They are not a subcontractor list we assemble per project. They are people we work with continuously, which is why we can put someone credible on the ground quickly, in the right language, without a hiring cycle.

For a client, the practical effect is this: you are not buying one consultant in Tokyo. You are buying a team that already spans the distance between your head office and your Japanese counterparty.

Working Alongside Public-Sector Support

Japan has strong public-sector support for inbound companies. JETRO, through its Invest Japan Business Support Center, provides incorporation, tax and labour advice, PR support, business matching and office search - and many of the companies we work with have used these services to good effect.

Our role sits alongside that, and covers different ground:
