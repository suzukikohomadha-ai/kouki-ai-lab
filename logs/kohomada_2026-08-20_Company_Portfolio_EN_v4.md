## Who We Are

Kohomada is a Japanese company that helps two kinds of clients move in opposite directions:

- **Overseas companies that want to sell in Japan**, and
- **Japanese companies that want to sell abroad.**

We are not a research firm that hands over a report and steps back. We stay on the project — finding the right counterparts, setting up the meetings, sitting in the negotiation room with you in both Japanese and English, and supporting the contract and trade work through to the close.

We are a small, senior team — **Naoya Takamatsu, CEO**, and **【TBC: COO氏名】, COO** — working with a network of specialists and partners in Japan and abroad. You deal with the principals directly, not with an account manager.

---

## What We Do

### 1. Japan Market Entry
For overseas companies entering or expanding in the Japanese market.

- Market and competitor research
- Identification and shortlisting of target companies
- **Direct outreach and meeting arrangement**
- **Attendance at negotiations, with Japanese–English support**
- Distribution channel and reseller development
- Contract and trade / import-export practicalities

### 2. Overseas Expansion
The same support, in the opposite direction, for Japanese companies going abroad.

- Market research and local partner identification in the target country
- Meeting arrangement and negotiation support on the ground
- Export / import operations

### 3. AI & Business Automation
Process automation, offered alongside the above or on its own.

- Business process mapping and automation design
- Workflow implementation (n8n and similar platforms)
- AI-assisted research and document production

## Completed Engagements

### Sri Lanka — Exclusive Manufacturing Agreement & Launch of Our Own Brand in Japan

Coconut husk was being discarded as industrial waste in Sri Lanka. We concluded an **exclusive agreement with a Sri Lankan manufacturer**, had the husk processed into a usable material, and brought it to the Japanese market under **our own brand, CocoGS** — sold today in Japan as a horticultural soil conditioner and pet bedding material, including on Amazon.

This is our own product line rather than client work, and it shows the full path we can run for others: **securing an exclusive agreement with an overseas producer, and building the route to the Japanese end customer.**

## How We Differ from Public-Sector Support

Public agencies such as JETRO provide valuable support for companies entering Japan, and we regularly work alongside their programmes. Our role is different, and complementary:

| | Public agencies | Kohomada |
|---|---|---|
| **Nature** | Public institution | **Private company** |
| **Getting started** | Structured programmes and procedures | **Low barrier to entry — we can move immediately** |
| **Scope** | Information provision and business matching | **From meeting arrangement through negotiation to closing** |
| **Continuity** | Within programme timeframes | **Continuous, project-based accompaniment** |
